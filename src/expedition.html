      Expedition field guide

      Five landmarks: Launch Garden, Wind Gap, The Fork, Second Chance and Observatory. Wide crossings need deliberate steering. The violet outer spring at The Fork is an optional shortcut with an 8-bounty shard. A lower cyan wall in Second Chance can rescue a missed catch. Broad ledges let you rest; there is no timer or final roof.

      A fresh catch during a fall can save a life before the world-space fall boundary. Public play has three lives. Test builds also offer unlimited Practice.         How bounty, Flow and lives work

        Gold shards earn bounty. Spend it on skill unlocks or camp services. Surge spends 26 Flow for a dash, separate from skill recharge. Perfect catches earn more Flow; neither currency is a life. Gold walls save the place you return to after losing a life.

        Public runs have 3 lives. Your height record and bounty survive Game Over. Optional camp contracts risk only their stated deposit, and marked directional rings need you to cross in the arrow's direction.

        Hollow gold shards above central ledges pay 1 bounty; wall-side shards pay 2 or 4. Both count for Courier, so the high route can finish its goal without diving below the ledges.

        In the new course, skills recharge by climbing: a PERFECT fresh catch restores 3 points, GOOD restores 2, other catches 1; a shard restores 1 and a directional ring 4. Waiting does not refill skills. Contracts can grant a ready skill, one Overdrive activation or a life.
