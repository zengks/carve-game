Expedition field guide

Move and jump toward a glowing edge. Hold CARVE to grip it, then release when the charge ring turns gold to launch higher. On a keyboard, use A/D or the arrows to steer, Space/W to jump, Z to carve, X for Flow Surge, and C for your equipped skill. This keeps your left hand together while your right hand uses the arrows. Prefer A/D? J (carve), K (surge) and L (skill) also work; Shift and Q remain available.

With touch controls enabled, hold a direction with one thumb and use JUMP, CARVE and SURGE with the other. Hold CARVE before reaching a wall to catch it.

Clean releases build Flow and help you chain launches. Momentum carries between jumps; steer against your movement to brake.

Gold beacons save your recovery point. Cyan bridges are one-way; gold chevrons mark springboards. Explore sideways to find richer shards and colored wind or lift lanes. Every 100 m and each contract earns bounty. Press C / L / Q or tap your skill to climb faster; unlock more in the Workshop. Purple rifts are optional: press E nearby to see their rules before entering. There is no roof. Pause and bank your run whenever you like. Records, bounty and unlocks stay on this browser.
